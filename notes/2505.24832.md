# How much do language models memorize?

## One-line takeaway

## Problem

Studying memorization in language models is a problem of 2 factors: (1) unintended memorization for one dataset and (2) generalization across datasets. Apparently past studies have shown that an induced model to generate a piece of text it processed multiple times is not memorization necessarily. In other words, the authors are in the lookout for a better proxy of memorizing.&#x20;

And apparently, there's work that suggests that memorization is an effect of unintended memorization in a specific area.&#x20;
