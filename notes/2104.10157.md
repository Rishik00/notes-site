# VideoGPT: Video Generation using VQ-VAE and Transformers

* A video foundation model co authored by aravind srinivas (why do all researchers end up as founders lmao)
* According to the abstract:

> The model uses VQ-VAEs to learn downsampled latent representations of a raw video by employing 3D convolutions and axial self-attention
> not sure what that means.

* Apparently, video models are important (both conditional and unconditional) for video production and forecasting. And there have been theories that video models implicitly learn geometries and physics.
* Apparently there are 2 types of video models: (1) Likelihood based (NVAE, VQ-VAE, iGPT) that are convinient to train and easy to work with, and (2) generation based (GANs).
* Hmm, this is interesting. So, VideoGPT has 2 components: (1) The VQ-VAE (encoder-decoder where each thing consists of 3D convolutions to downsample + attention residual blocks) that is used for learning the representations into discrete latent samples, which are then sent down to an autoregressive GPT style transformer.
* And they tried this on a bunch of datasets that I don't know much about. I guess we have to go deeper wrt the architecture.
